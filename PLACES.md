## 02 · Tokyo 東京

Wed 23 Dec – Mon 28 Dec, 5 nights

- [Kamakura](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kamakura%2C%20Japan) 鎌倉 · City · Seaside temple town, about 1 hour from Tokyo.
- [Tokyo](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tokyo%2C%20Japan) 東京 · City · Huge city. Stay near Shinjuku or Shibuya so the JR Yamanote loop line is on your doorstep.
- [Yokohama](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yokohama%2C%20Japan) 横浜 · City · Chinatown, Minato Mirai waterfront, Cup Noodles Museum.
- [Ghibli Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ghibli%20Museum%2C%20Japan) 三鷹の森ジブリ美術館 · Sight · Timed tickets go on sale the month before and sell out fast.
- [Kamakura Great Buddha](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kamakura%20Great%20Buddha%2C%20Japan) 鎌倉大仏 · Sight · Giant bronze Buddha at Kotoku-in.
- [Meiji Jingu](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Meiji%20Jingu%2C%20Japan) 明治神宮 · Sight · Forest shrine next to Harajuku. Calm early in the morning.
- [Odaiba](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Odaiba%2C%20Japan) お台場 · Sight · Waterfront with the life-size Gundam and Rainbow Bridge views.
- [Senso-ji](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Senso-ji%2C%20Japan) 浅草寺 · Sight · Tokyo's oldest temple, in Asakusa. Quietest before 8am or after dark.
- [Shibuya Crossing](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shibuya%20Crossing%2C%20Japan) 渋谷スクランブル交差点 · Sight · The famous scramble crossing outside Shibuya Station.
- [Shibuya Sky](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shibuya%20Sky%2C%20Japan) 渋谷スカイ · Sight · Open-air rooftop on Shibuya Scramble Square. Sunset slots sell out, so book ahead.
- [Shinjuku](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shinjuku%2C%20Japan) 新宿 · Sight · Neon streets, Omoide Yokocho alleys, Golden Gai bars, the Godzilla head in Kabukicho.
- [teamLab Planets](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=teamLab%20Planets%2C%20Japan) チームラボプラネッツ · Sight · Walk-through digital art; you wade through water barefoot. Book tickets ahead.
- [Tokyo Disneyland](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tokyo%20Disneyland%2C%20Japan) 東京ディズニーランド · Sight
- [Tokyo DisneySea](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tokyo%20DisneySea%2C%20Japan) 東京ディズニーシー · Sight
- [Tokyo Skytree](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tokyo%20Skytree%2C%20Japan) 東京スカイツリー · Sight · 634 m tower, about 20 minutes on foot from Senso-ji.
- [Tokyo Tower](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tokyo%20Tower%2C%20Japan) 東京タワー · Sight · The classic red-and-white tower.
- [Ueno Park](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ueno%20Park%2C%20Japan) 上野公園 · Sight · Museums and the zoo, with Ameyoko market next door.
- [Imperial Palace East Gardens](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Imperial%20Palace%20East%20Gardens%2C%20Japan) 皇居東御苑 · Nature · Free gardens on the old Edo Castle grounds.
- [Shinjuku Gyoen](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shinjuku%20Gyoen%2C%20Japan) 新宿御苑 · Nature · Large landscaped garden. Famous for cherry blossoms.
- [Tsukiji Outer Market](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tsukiji%20Outer%20Market%2C%20Japan) 築地場外市場 · Food · Seafood breakfast and street snacks. Best before 11am.
- [Yanaka Ginza](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yanaka%20Ginza%2C%20Japan) 谷中銀座 · Food · Old-Tokyo shopping street with snack stalls.
- [Akihabara](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Akihabara%2C%20Japan) 秋葉原 · Shopping · Electronics, anime, figures, retro games, maid cafes.
- [Ikebukuro](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ikebukuro%2C%20Japan) 池袋 · Shopping · Big anime shops, Sunshine City and Otome Road.
- [Nakano Broadway](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nakano%20Broadway%2C%20Japan) 中野ブロードウェイ · Shopping · Old mall packed with anime, manga and collectibles shops.
- [Shimokitazawa](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Shimokitazawa%2C%20Japan) 下北沢 · Shopping · Vintage clothes, record shops, small cafes.
- [Takeshita Street](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Takeshita%20Street%2C%20Japan) 竹下通り · Shopping · Harajuku street fashion, crepes and candy floss.
- [Haneda Airport](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Haneda%20Airport%2C%20Japan) 羽田空港 · Airport · Close to the city: about 30 minutes to central Tokyo.

## 03 · Hakone 箱根

Mon 28 Dec – Tue 29 Dec, 1 night

- [Chureito Pagoda](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chureito%20Pagoda%2C%20Japan) 忠霊塔 · Sight · The postcard view: five-storey pagoda with Mt Fuji behind.
- [Hakone Shrine](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hakone%20Shrine%2C%20Japan) 箱根神社 · Sight · Red torii standing in Lake Ashi.
- [Kawaguchiko](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kawaguchiko%2C%20Japan) 河口湖 · Nature · Lake at the foot of Mt Fuji.
- [Mount Fuji](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mount%20Fuji%2C%20Japan) 富士山 · Nature · Climbing season is roughly July to early September.
- [Owakudani](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Owakudani%2C%20Japan) 大涌谷 · Nature · Steaming volcanic valley. Try the black eggs.
- [Hakone](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hakone%2C%20Japan) 箱根 · Onsen · Hot-spring town: Lake Ashi, Owakudani, ropeway, Mt Fuji views on clear days.

## 04 · Kyoto 京都

Tue 29 Dec – Fri 1 Jan, 3 nights

- [Kyoto](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kyoto%2C%20Japan) 京都 · City · Friend's tip: buses are slow and crowded. Use trains (JR, Keihan, Hankyu, subway) and walk.
- [Fushimi Inari Taisha](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fushimi%20Inari%20Taisha%2C%20Japan) 伏見稲荷大社 · Sight · Thousands of red torii up the mountain. Go around 7am.
- [Ginkaku-ji](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ginkaku-ji%2C%20Japan) 銀閣寺 · Sight · Silver Pavilion and its moss garden.
- [Gion](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gion%2C%20Japan) 祇園 · Sight · Geisha district. Hanamikoji street at dusk.
- [Kinkaku-ji](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kinkaku-ji%2C%20Japan) 金閣寺 · Sight · The Golden Pavilion.
- [Kiyomizu-dera](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kiyomizu-dera%2C%20Japan) 清水寺 · Sight · Wooden stage temple; walk down Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka after.
- [Nijo Castle](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nijo%20Castle%2C%20Japan) 二条城 · Sight · Shogun's palace with squeaking nightingale floors.
- [Sanjusangen-do](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sanjusangen-do%2C%20Japan) 三十三間堂 · Sight · Hall of 1,001 Kannon statues.
- [Tenryu-ji](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tenryu-ji%2C%20Japan) 天龍寺 · Sight · Zen temple and garden right next to the bamboo grove.
- [Arashiyama Bamboo Grove](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Arashiyama%20Bamboo%20Grove%2C%20Japan) 嵐山竹林 · Nature · Go early, before the tour groups.
- [Philosopher's Path](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Philosopher's%20Path%2C%20Japan) 哲学の道 · Nature · Canal-side walk between Ginkaku-ji and Nanzen-ji.
- [Nishiki Market](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nishiki%20Market%2C%20Japan) 錦市場 · Food · Covered food market, good for lunch grazing.

**One Piece & Jujutsu Kaisen**

- [Kyoto Tower](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=34.98753%2C135.75936) 京都タワー · Jujutsu Kaisen · Appears in a manga flashback (chapter 17). Right across from Kyoto Station.
- [Mugiwara Store Mini Kyoto](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kiddyland+Kyoto+Shijo+Kawaramachi) 麦わらストア MINI 京都 · One Piece · A small One Piece corner inside Kiddyland Kyoto, Shijo-Kawaramachi. Usually 11:00–21:00.

## 05 · Nara 奈良

Fri 1 Jan

- [Nara](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nara%2C%20Japan) 奈良 · City · Old capital, easy from Kyoto or Osaka.
- [Kasuga Taisha](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kasuga%20Taisha%2C%20Japan) 春日大社 · Sight · Shrine lined with thousands of lanterns.
