    Where we sleep

    Paste a Google Maps or booking link for each place. Keep a few as Options while you compare, and mark the one you book as Booked. Places to sleep show on the map as pink bed pins, separate from the route.

    Short links from the Google Maps app (maps.app.goo.gl) don't contain the location. Keep the place name on the line before or after the link, or tap the map to place it afterwards.

        Add a database

        Works with a CSV (columns like name, link, lat, lng, category, note), a Google Takeout Saved Places.json, a Google My Maps KML export, or a text file of Google Maps links.

        A Google Sheet works too: File, Share, Publish to web, choose CSV, and paste that link. Press Refresh on it any time to read the latest rows.
